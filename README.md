# -CodeSprint-2026-ERROR-404
Official project repository for Team ERROR 404 at CODESPRINT 2026 — unified final codebase for our team submission.
UPI Autopay & Hidden Deduction Detector is a privacy-first web application designed to stop silent money leaks from bank accounts and digital wallets. Users often lose money to forgotten UPI Autopay mandates, auto-renewing free trials, and sneaky one-time bank or platform fees buried inside cluttered SMS inboxes. This dashboard parses bank and UPI SMS alerts using regex and keyword matching to extract merchant names, amounts, debit reasons, and countdown timelines. It flags urgent upcoming debits within three days, tracks hidden charges, provides step-by-step cancellation guides for major UPI apps, and uses client-side Tesseract.js OCR to verify cancellation screenshots locally.
